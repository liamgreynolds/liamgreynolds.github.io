Buzz Networks

I made this poster for a climate and development class. Inspired by Actor Network Theory, I wanted to create a visualization of an actor-network system — mapping the network of Uganda’s smallholder coffee industry as farms adapt to the impacts of climate change. It traces economic flows of coffee from smallholder farms through cooperatives, middlemen, and roasters, alongside the climate adaptation connections helping farmers respond to a changing climate.
