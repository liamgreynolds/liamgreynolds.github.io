Great Chebeague Island

I’ve been going to Great Chebeague Island for over 10 years now, and I’ve lived there several summers. It holds a great place in my heart, and there wasn’t a well-developed map of the area — so I decided to make one.

This map was made in QGIS and Adobe Illustrator, although most of the labeling was placed by hand in Illustrator, as many of the ponds and other small features are too small to exist in publicly available GIS files. As a result, much of this map was actually made by hand — including the beaches, which I traced from satellite imagery, since they weren’t included in Maine’s state beach survey and I couldn’t get an accurate land classification for sand from satellite imagery.
