Drive to Survive

I made this map for Data Fest this past year, in collaboration with three other students. It represents the total distance that patients with myeloid leukemia had to drive in order to receive care at the University of Kansas Topeka Health Center, part of the Stormont Vail Health Network in northeast Kansas. Working with a large dataset of patient information, I used network analysis — along with QGIS and Adobe Illustrator — to trace each patient’s travel across all of their visits. Each red flow line connects a patient’s home to the medical center, with line width scaled to total miles driven; some patients traveled over 7,000 miles.
