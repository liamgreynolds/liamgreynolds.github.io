Vermont Landslides Pamphlet

A 12-page public information pamphlet, “A Vermonter’s Guide to Landslide Hazards,” developed with Middlebury College. Combining maps, diagrams, and photographs, it explains where and why landslides occur in Vermont and how residents can recognize and reduce their risk.
