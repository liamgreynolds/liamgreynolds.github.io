Myeloid Leukemia and Healthcare Coverage in Kansas

A group project with Aidan DeLorenzo, Hayden Herrick, Owen Ou, and Riley Foushee examining myeloid leukemia mortality and healthcare coverage in Kansas. Using CDC WONDER data on myeloid leukemia mortality from 1999 to 2020, we mapped how rates vary across the state: Kansas sits among the higher states nationally, and rates run highest in its more rural areas, far from the specialized hospital care that treatment requires. The presentation walks through the disease and its treatment, the mortality patterns, and what healthcare coverage looks like for the patients affected.
